## Kent Nutrition vs. Kent brand naming clarification

On purpose, a strategic decision was made that the Kent Nutrition company shares a logo and look/feel virtually identical to the Kent brand (the commercial brand) — which is good, but can also create some confusion. Ties back to legacy: "Kent Feeds" as a company name isn't used anymore — Kent still makes animal feeds, but "Kent Feeds" the company doesn't exist. The DBA (Doing Business As) is Kent Nutrition, and within Kent Nutrition's brands, Kent is one of those brands. Legally that's not the exact name, but for what people search for, wherever it needs to show up on the Kent Worldwide side is fine either way.
